I am {% include phd_year.liquid start=page.phd_start %} PhD student at the [University of Washington Information School](https://ischool.uw.edu/), working with [Benjamin Charles Germain Lee](https://bcglee.github.io/). My research sits at the intersection of digital libraries, information retrieval, and computational humanities.

I study how multimodal machine learning can make large cultural heritage and government collections, from digitized maps and photographs to web archives, discoverable when their metadata is sparse or missing.
